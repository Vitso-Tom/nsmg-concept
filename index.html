The Bands

The artists that make the North Shore scene what it is. Want your band featured? Join the Guild.

Bare Bones
High Energy Rock / Covers

A 4-piece band with 4 lead vocalists, based on the North Shore. Bare Bones performs on a regular basis and plays a wide variety of highly recognizable covers and fan favorites. Available for any type of venue and private event.

Facebook
Ordinary Madness
Progressive Rock / Classic Covers

A North Shore band playing covers spanning five decades -- from the iconic anthems to the hidden gems seldom heard by other cover bands. With over two decades of musical camaraderie, the members of Ordinary Madness share a bond as enduring as the songs they play. From progressive rock gems to danceable rock and roll classics of the 70s, 80s, 90s, and 00s, they're here to ignite your senses and take you on a musical journey like no other.

Fred Ellsworth
Country / Rock

A country artist from Westminster who will draw you in with his guitar and keep you with his soaring vocals. Equally at home with Haggard and Cash as with Petty and Skynyrd, Fred has spent years opening for the likes of Marshal Tucker, Leann Rimes, Tim McGraw, Faith Hill, Willie Nelson, and Brad Paisley, developing a contagious sense of ease and fun in front of any size crowd.

About

Why the Guild Exists

The North Shore has always had a live music scene. From the dive bars in Lynn to the listening rooms in Newburyport, there are people playing every night of the week. But there's never been a single place to find out about it all.

The North Shore Musicians Guild is here to change that. We're building a community hub where fans can discover shows, bands can find gigs and connect with each other, and venues can reach the audience that's already out there looking for live music.

Not a booking agency. Not a record label. A community resource, run by someone who loves this scene and wants to see it thrive. If you play, listen, or host live music on the North Shore -- this is for you.

-- The Guild

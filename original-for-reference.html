Building Community Note by Note

North Shore Musicians Guild is a community-driven initiative focused on supporting local musicians, their fans, and the venues that host live music. By creating connections, sharing insight, and promoting collaboration, the Guild aims to strengthen the North Shore live music scene and foster a vibrant local music culture.
